About me

I like the start of things.

I'm Swati, a product marketer in New York. I'm happiest at the beginning: figuring out the quirks, finding what actually hurts, and experimenting my way to something I can point at.

Right now that's Heron Data (YC S20), where I'm the only marketer at a 60-person company. I own positioning, launches, pricing and PR, including our Series A announcement.

Before that I was in restructuring at Alvarez & Marsal, which is where I figured out I wanted to be on the revenue side.
