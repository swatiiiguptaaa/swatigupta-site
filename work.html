01

Product positioning

Deciding what we sell, to whom, and why we win, before anyone writes a headline.

Core narrative · 2025

One story, everywhere

Problem. Heron sold to three different buyers (lenders, brokers, insurers) with three different pitches, and the website, deck and emails each said something else.

What I did. Rebuilt positioning from customer interviews, win/loss reviews and competitive research, then rolled one core narrative out across web, sales deck, email and social, with a variant for each segment.

Result. One story sales and marketing both tell, and the foundation for every launch since.

Packaging & pricing · Q4 2026

What we actually sell

Problem. A growing catalogue of products and SKUs with no shared logic for how they bundle or price.

What I did. Inventoried every product and SKU, ran co-usage and attach-rate analysis across the customer base, and built the value ladder the packages hang on: auto-decline → auto-offer → auto-learn. Ran working sessions with squad leads, customer success and the founders.

pricing is positioning with a number on it

New vertical · 2025

Insurance, from zero

Problem. Heron wanted to expand into specialty insurance with no ICP or playbook.

What I did. Ran 50+ discovery interviews, defined the ICP and segments, mapped the competitive landscape and wrote the go-to-market playbook.

Result. The playbook the insurance expansion runs on today.

The kit sellers actually use

What I built. Competitive battle cards, ICP one-pagers, seller FAQs, demo storylines and a collateral library, plus automated lead routing and messaging so every inbound lands with the right rep and context.

Result. Demo show rate up to 67%.

Proof system

Value, in the customer's numbers

What I built. A value-metrics framework across the seven workflows customers run on Heron, each with headline, operational and economic metrics. It gives sales, case studies and customer reporting one vocabulary for ROI.

Plus. Customer case studies and technical release notes for every launch.

Customer comms · Oct 2026

A launch nobody hears about didn't happen

What I built. How existing customers hear about what's new: P1/P2/P3 release tiers, a shared definition of "ready," and a broadcast-then-targeted follow-up loop with customer ops. First run: the Fraud Consortium launch.
